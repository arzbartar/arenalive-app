## 📱 About Arena Live

**Arena Live** is a free Android IPTV-style streaming app for watching **live football matches, sports TV channels and event replays** — built for Persians and football fans living abroad. Stream in HD with a native media player (libmpv / media_kit), pick your video quality, switch subtitles, and get **push notifications** when your match starts.

- 🌍 Works anywhere — designed for users **outside Iran** (Iranians abroad)
- ⚡ Native **HLS (m3u8)** playback with low latency
- 🔒 Secure streams — links are never exposed, playback goes through our protected proxy
